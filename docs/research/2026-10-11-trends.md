## 0. Ten things that matter most this week

1. **Oct 1, 2026: YouTube Shorts recommendations now favour original content and cut reach for re-uploads/aggregators; "minor technical edits or template-based changes don't count as original" (Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie).** Monetisation stays under the separate reused-content policy. [snippet]
2. **Sept 23, 2026: "Shorts series" launched** — Shorts grouped into seasons/episodes with custom thumbnails and sequential playback, on web/mobile/TV; reported as YPP-only, Shorts-only, each entry ≤180 s. This is the biggest format opportunity for an episodic animated-story channel in 2026. [snippet]
3. **Sept 24, 2026: Shorts over 1 minute with an active copyright claim are no longer auto-blocked** (YouTube Help), but the claim still applies, so rights holders take the revenue. Does not change our "original audio only" rule. [snippet]
4. **Aug 10, 2026 YPP change, effective Feb 1, 2027:** new applicants need 8,000 qualified watch hours/365 d or 20M qualified Shorts views/90 d; existing members must hold **10M Shorts views per rolling 90 d to keep Shorts revenue share** (it pauses below that); new "active channel" definitions; members must accept new terms by Jan 31, 2027. [snippet, one source says lower 500-sub tier unchanged]
5. **Aug 24, 2026: public view counts now count from first frame on all formats** (Shorts already did since Mar 31, 2025); the old stricter count is "engaged views" in Studio; YouTube says earnings, YPP eligibility and recommendations are unchanged. Third-party estimate: public counts ~30% higher. [snippet]
6. **July 13–16, 2026: "inauthentic content" monetisation policy clarified into three buckets** (generic/repetitive; unsatisfying/off-putting; fake AI "experts"), with "characters repeatedly placed in the same situation" given as a repetitive example and review done at the channel-pattern level. Animation channels were demonetised/terminated in the Jan–Feb 2026 sweep (16 channels, ~4.7B views; a ~6M-sub Dragon Ball-themed animation channel; kaiju/dinosaur animation channels). [snippet]
7. **May 27, 2026: AI labels made more visible and auto-applied for photorealistic content** (overlay on Shorts); stylised/animated content is reported as exempt unless realistic; permanent labels for Veo/Dream Screen/C2PA-"fully generative" files; disputes in Studio. [snippet]
8. **Roblox this week:** the live-player leader is an egg-hatching/pet-raiding "steal-a-" game (~1.2–1.9M concurrent, Oct 7–10) that was briefly pulled in late August over an in-game AI-video "doomscroll treadmill", came back without it, and triggered a Roblox rule banning "continuous feed" reward loops for younger accounts. Fastest-growing lists show a woods-survival game (~319K), a mascot-horror alpha (~177K) and an anime-style fighter (~121K). [snippet, counts conflict by tracker]
9. **Halloween is the dominant 4-week hook:** many big experiences launch in-game events on **Oct 17**; a platform-wide Halloween event is teased (leak: "The Hollow", Oct 22, unofficial); Halloween itself is **Saturday Oct 31**; then Nov 1 (Day of the Dead / US clocks back), Nov 5 Bonfire Night (UK), Nov 8 Diwali. [snippet + calendar]

Their *visual grammar* (low-angle costume-reveal walk, text-overlay-vs-vibe contrast, pumpkin-head illusion, "subtle foreshadowing" spliced-ending edit) is audio-independent and can be done with original SFX/beds. ## 5. Implications for the Blox Studio app (hypotheses, for the owner to decide)

1. **Publisher/scheduler: add Shorts-series awareness** — season/episode fields, per-episode thumbnail, ≤180 s and Shorts-only validation, sequential ordering. [hyp from Sept 23 feature]
2. **Backlog "variation guard"** — a template-similarity score across recent episodes (premise, setting, cast, conflict type, resolution, pacing) with a hard rule against the same character in the same situation N times in a row; surfaces in QC. [hyp from July 2026 policy + Oct 1 originality push]
3. **Policy fields per video** — AI-disclosure default off for stylised animation, forced on if any realistic element or real-person likeness; MFK risk flag with explicit owner decision; branded-content flag. [hyp]
4. **Audio "trend adapter"** — catalogue audio-led trends as visual grammars (reveal-walk, overlay-contrast, spliced-ending) with original SFX/beds; keep all music original. [hyp]
5. **Metrics** — rank by engaged views and "stayed to watch", not public views; expect a ~30% public-view inflation artefact after Aug 24. [snippet + hyp]
6. **YPP runway planning** — track rolling 90-day qualified Shorts views against the 10M maintenance bar before Feb 1, 2027; enforce the 5-Shorts/90-day activity floor. [snippet + hyp]
7. **Seasonal feed** — pre-load the Section 4 calendar into the story backlog with generic nouns; prioritise Oct 17–31 Halloween slots and a Nov 1 "clocks back/extra hour" gag. [hyp]
8. **Backdrop vocabulary** — add generic nouns (egg-hatching pet-raid game, survive-the-nights woods, mascot toy world, fishing lake, dress-up runway, roleplay town, anime-style fighters, base-stealing) to the web characters/settings vocab so stories feel current without trademarks. [hyp]
