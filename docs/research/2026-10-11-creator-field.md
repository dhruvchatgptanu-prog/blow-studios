* **Zevireth** - fight choreography, VFX-heavy, game-IP driven (Blade Ball); 2-3 uploads/month; long-form compilations of Shorts (3:32, 12M views) show a second-use pattern worth noting.

## 4. Cross-creator patterns

### What the winners share (hypotheses from snippets; none verified frame-by-frame)
1. **Hook at second 0 and captions at second 0.** A creator who analysed the top 15 viral Roblox Shorts reported 15/15 had a hook at 0 s and 12/15 showed captions immediately; forum consensus is "something changes every ~2 s" (BlackHatWorld journey thread, Feb 2026).
2. **Ride the live game/meme wave fast, with a legible archetype.** Sillycat (Pro vs Rich on *Steal an Egg*), Piee (99 Nights deer), LankyBox (67 / *Steal a Brainrot* "tsunami" videos), Hawks (Skibidi). The archetypes are old (Noob/Pro/Rich, POV, "me vs bro"); the setting is new.
3. **Recurring characters or serialization.** Piee's "characters build a larger story", Hawks' "part 1/2/3", Davebb933's Noob, PMdamiann's skit universe. Serialization converts a viral Short into subscribers.
4. **Emotional conflict in the title + 1-2 emoji.** Rich vs poor, betrayal/scam ("He Stole Everything", "He Scammed Me"), rescue/sacrifice, twist warnings ("I should have ignored that call"), "POV:" framing.
5. **One of two cadence models:** volume (Hawks ~30/mo, ShanePlays every 2 days, TTS farms 1,000+ videos) **or** low-frequency/high-quality (Piee, Zevireth 2-3/mo). The middle ("3/week of generic") is where the stalled channels sit.
6. **Cross-posting to TikTok** (Hawks 1.5M, Piee 387K, PMdamiann, GH'S) as a second discovery surface.

### What the stalled channels do differently
* TTS over other people's/generic gameplay with no characters (Luca tier: 2.7K-14K avg views).
* Automation signatures: one journey channel was banned in week 2 for "bot/script activity" and capped at 1.4-1.8K views/video.
* Derivative IP (Skibidi re-animations) as the wave plateaus; "6-7" is listed as "heavily declining" in 2026.
* Long-form-native storytelling pushed into Shorts without re-cutting for the format (ShanePlays Shorts avg 116K vs 5.2M subs).
* AI-slop look-alikes: Kapwing's new-account audit found 21% of the first 500 Shorts were AI slop and 33% "brainrot" (late 2025); YouTube's CEO named cutting AI slop a 2026 priority; Roblox itself removed *Steal an Egg* in Aug 2026 for gen-AI "brainrot scrolling". Being visibly hand-made/original is now a positioning advantage.

### Emerging formats this month (Sept-Oct 2026)
* "Pro vs Rich" / "Poor vs Rich" on the newest game (Sillycat).
* *99 Nights in the Forest* animations (deer/survival POV); the game had a September breakout, a 45M-view TikTok, and a Fortnite crossover on Oct 8 - the setting is hot across platforms.
* MM2 "Eclipser" role-animation edits (trend page updated Sept 14) and a "Pad Thai" meme animation (early Oct, 79.5K likes): short meme-sound animation beats.
* Meta-commentary Shorts ("POV: Brainrot is FINALLY DYING...") - brainrot fatigue as a story angle.
* Interactive/participation formats (tier lists, Akinator) on Ninye.
* Platform-side: YouTube's **1 Oct 2026 Shorts originality update** (original content favoured; reposts/"voice-over describing someone else's clip" do not count as original; recommendations only, monetisation unchanged), on top of the July 2025 reused-content monetisation rule. Max Short length remains 3 min (since 15 Oct 2024); vendor studies cluster the best-performing length at 30-60 s with retention/completion weighted.
* Current game settings (Oct 1 live chart): Brookhaven RP #1 (~210K in-game), with *Steal a Brainrot* (25M CCU record), *Grow a Garden* (22.3M peak Aug 2025), *99 Nights* (14.2M peak), *Murder Mystery 2* (700K+ peaks) as the big story backdrops.

## 5. Fit for a Blender block-character pipeline (Blox Studio)

**Can do well (original characters, offline voices, captions, SFX):**
1. **Serialized micro-stories with a recurring cast** (the Piee/narrow/Davebb933 pattern): recurring originals, each Short a complete beat, titles that promise an emotional turn. The app's costume/expression/snap-cut/zoom-punch features map directly onto this.
2. **"A vs B" comparison skits with original archetypes** (noob/pro/rich/poor, "me vs bro") in original settings; RobloxArtSchool shows Blender handles the format; adding story and characters is the gap.
3. **"POV:" relatable skits** (PMdamiann pattern): one situation, 20-40 s, snap expressions, caption at 0 s.
4. **Twist/betrayal/rescue mini-dramas** that the TTS tier does with footage - done instead with animated original characters and natural offline voices, which is now algorithmically favoured over narration-over-clips.
5. **Generic hot-setting parodies** (survival forest night, garden/farm, egg or pet theft, roleplay-town rich vs poor) built from original assets, never from game assets or named game/brand characters.
6. **Fast meme-cycle response using original audio**: short beat-driven gags timed to the app's own SFX/music rather than trending licensed sounds.

**Cannot / should not do:**
* IP re-animations (Skibidi, Sushi Monsters, brainrot mascots, 67 characters), trending licensed audio memes, or in-game "Trending Meme Animations" assets: originality, licensing and the plateau all argue against.
* Blade Ball-grade fight choreography and VFX (Zevireth): beyond a deterministic rig and outside the originality brief.
* Interactive/participation formats (tier lists, Akinator, live reactions) and live narrated gameplay (Poke/Hyper/Flamingo).
* Long-form cast dramas (ShanePlays/ObliviousHD) for now; a serialized arc of Shorts is the pipeline-native substitute.
